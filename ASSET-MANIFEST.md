## Display rules

- The Try-On selector loads the selected item’s own image. When a separate garment-detail concept image exists, it is used in the canvas while the selection summary displays the original campaign/reference image alongside it. No generic silhouette is used as a fallback for the selected product.
- External-brand images are isolated in their own reference group and labelled as inspiration only, not BLACKED inventory or an authorized collaboration. The site does not link to or embed the source site. Check permission before public commercial use.
- Customer photo preview remains browser-local. The optional illustration guide is generic and does not extract or fit the selected garment.

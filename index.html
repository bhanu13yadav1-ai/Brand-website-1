GOOD TO KNOW

Clear answers.
Better choices.

Can I select all 17 style options in Try-On Studio? +

Yes. Pick any of the ten original references, four BLACKED design concepts, or three separately labelled SYRN style-inspiration drawings. The canvas shows the selected reference image; the four new BLACKED concepts also include product-detail photos. The optional personal-photo guide is generic—not an exact garment cut-out or a fit prediction.

Why does Try-On show a product photo now?+

The canvas defaults to the exact selected reference image, so selecting a different reference changes the main preview. The illustration guide is separate and only becomes available after a personal photo is selected; it does not extract the garment from the campaign artwork.

Does the manual overlay show my actual fit?+

No. It lets you manually position a simple illustration over your own photo. It does not use the selected product image as a garment cut-out, change to match body shape, simulate fabric or determine if a product fits.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?+

This prototype uses your selected photo inside the browser for previewing and does not send it to a server. Only select photos you have permission to use. If the website is later connected to third-party services, the privacy notice must be updated first.

Are these all confirmed products for sale?+

No. The ten items are your original uploaded images. Some are BLACKED campaign visuals; others are design references, and a few visibly contain other-brand labels or social-media UI. Do not present those as BLACKED stock unless you own/are permitted to use the creative and have confirmed the actual product, construction, sizes and inventory.

Can the size finder guarantee the right size?+

No. It uses an editable sample chart to demonstrate the sizing flow. It must be replaced with measurements from actual BLACKED products before customers rely on it for orders.

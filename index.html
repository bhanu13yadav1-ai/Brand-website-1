CLOTHING MEASUREMENTS

Measure carefully. Choose with context.

Centimetres ↔ inches

Exact length conversion only. This does not calculate a garment size.

90 cm = 35.43 in
Clothing size charts differ between brands, garment types and countries. Use the seller's verified chart. If between sizes, check fabric stretch, fit notes and the actual return policy.

Fit checklist

  • Use a flexible measuring tape without pulling it tight.
  • Take measurements over light clothing or as the product guide instructs.
  • Measure bust at the fullest point and underbust just below the bust, keeping the tape level.
  • For briefs, use the product's waist/hip measurement instructions.
  • Stop wearing any garment that causes pain, numbness, skin injury or persistent discomfort.

BODY EDUCATION

Anatomy, myths & when to seek help.

Vulva vs. vagina

The vulva is the external genital area; it includes the labia, clitoris, urethral opening and vaginal opening. The vagina is the internal muscular canal connecting the vaginal opening to the cervix. They are related but not interchangeable terms.

Cleveland Clinic: Vulva anatomy ↗ · Vagina anatomy ↗
Hymen and “virginity testing” myths

Virginity is not a medical diagnosis. The appearance of the hymen cannot reliably establish sexual history, and no examination can prove whether someone has had sex. Do not insert objects or fingers to try to determine this.

WHO interagency statement ↗
External hygiene basics

Gentle external washing with water and mild, unscented products is usually sufficient. Avoid douching and scented products if they cause irritation. The vagina cleans itself; aggressive internal cleaning can disrupt its normal balance.

Cleveland Clinic: care guidance ↗
When to ask a clinician

Persistent pain, unusual bleeding, sores, a strong or unusual change in discharge or odour, fever, or other concerning changes may need medical assessment. This guide cannot diagnose the cause. Seek urgent care for severe symptoms or heavy bleeding.

Choose a qualified healthcare professional who respects your privacy and answers your questions.

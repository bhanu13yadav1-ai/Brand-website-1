GOOD TO KNOW

Clear answers.

Is this a live online store? +

Not yet. The catalogue, local wishlist, cart demo and preview are front-end features. No real inventory, ordering, checkout or payment provider is connected.

Are the personal-wellness products available to buy? +

They are concept catalogue entries until product details, suppliers, materials, dimensions, pricing and stock are confirmed by the site owner.

Does the Fit Studio use AI? +

No. Reference mode shows the selected campaign/product reference. Manual overlay requires your photo and a transparent PNG garment cut-out that you supply; it positions that exact uploaded cut-out and does not predict fit. Realistic AI try-on is a separate future integration.

Is my photo uploaded? +

The preview code reads the chosen image locally in your browser. It does not send the image to a server.

How should personal-wellness products be selected? +

Read the manufacturer's usage, cleaning and storage instructions. Verify material safety and dimensions, use products only as intended, and stop use if discomfort or pain occurs. Anal toys should have a secure flared base designed to prevent full insertion.

BLACKED — INTIMATES + WELLNESS
Version: 1.9
Type: Self-contained responsive static HTML storefront prototype
Main features: separate Intimates / After Dark 18+ / Women's Wellness tabs; Home, Fit Studio; interactive eight-frame 360 angle viewer; concept catalogue; client-side wishlist, compare and demo bag; optional wellness comfort finder; local manual garment cut-out preview.
Core hero, product cards and 360° images are embedded in index.html, so runtime does not depend on uploading an assets directory.
Limitations: generated/cropped concept imagery; actual product images, stock, prices, payment, shipping, verified specs and realistic AI try-on are not connected.
